# Studio rules — Benji's taste

This file is Benji's. The studio reads it every night and it overrides the role files.
Add a line whenever you catch yourself giving the same feedback twice.
(Starter defaults written during setup — edit or delete anything that isn't actually you.)

## Audience
- Games are for me (an adult developer who likes puzzle and strategy games).
- Logic puzzles in the family of Suguru/Tectonic are a favourite.

## Always
- Every game must be playable on my phone and on desktop.
- Instant restart. Undo in any puzzle game.
- Clean, readable visuals over busy ones.

## Never
- No ads, fake currencies, energy systems or other dark patterns.
- No mandatory timers unless the idea is about time pressure.

## Studio habits
- Small and finished beats big and broken.
- When unsure about my intent, make a choice, write it down, and ask me in the report.
