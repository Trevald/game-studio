A playable look at learning on the board, candy chain reactions, and little reasons to come back. Same candy world. More delight.

The candy cabinet

Recognizable before you read.

Patterns carry the meaning. Every special keeps a clear number; effects preview before committing a chain.

Mix two. Make a moment.

Combinations activate only when both specials are selected in the same valid chain. Previewed blast tiles never contribute to the sum. These are proposed mechanics, not changes to the live game.

Small helps & happy surprises

A treat, never a transaction.

Level-granted tools and move-based bonuses. No inventory economy, shop, or real-time expiry.

🍭

Lollipop Hammer

Pick a candy, preview, smash. One charge in selected levels. Opens up the board without completing a chain for you.

⇄

Candy Swap

Exchange two candies. Show both destinations before confirming. A setup move for a satisfying long chain.

↝

Sugar Shuffle

A fresh arrangement of the same candies. Keep every special and guarantee at least one valid chain.

🍒

Cherry on Top

Add one to a candy. Preview 4 → 5. Maximum 9; unavailable targets look quiet rather than failing after selection.

⑂

Licorice Split

Later experiment: split one value across two cells. Needs a dedicated board rule; not part of the first release.

✦

Golden Batch

Three gold-rimmed candies last for three valid moves. Each adds a small score bonus when cleared. No countdown.

One bonus system first

Sugar Rush is the recommended first bonus. Golden Batch, Rainbow Round, a gentle Sweet Streak, Mystery Sweets, and a candy companion stay in the idea cupboard until the core experience is tested. More delight should not mean more rules at once.
