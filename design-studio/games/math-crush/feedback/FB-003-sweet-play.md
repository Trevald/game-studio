## Reviewer (separate agent) and unresolved questions for the designer
Verdict "ship with fixes". Fixed: Submit off-screen in tap mode, Hint, sticker toast carry-over. Not ours to change, raised here:
1. **Tension.** Every level has exactly two spare moves, every valid chain counts and the board is always repaired, so levels are only lost with charged wrong sums. The reviewer cleared all 12 taking the shortest chain each time, and got ★★★ on 11/12 taking the longest. Only three targets (10/12/15) across 12 levels.
2. **Hammer has no job.** With a solvable board and a quota that ignores which chain you make, there is never a reason to smash. Goals the hammer helps with (blockers, a specific candy to clear, fewer moves) would give it one.
3. **Perfect Batch** is automatic on L1–L5 (no tool exists there), so it unlocks with Sweet Start on the first win. Count it only on hammer levels?
4. Same-colour candies for different values (1/7, 2/8, 3/9 share art) may mislead players used to colour matching.
