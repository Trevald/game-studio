How to play

  1. Walk into a die to push it one square. You can't pull, and you can't push two at once.
  2. The die tips over as it moves: the side you push against becomes the top.
  3. The small numbers around a die are its side faces. Opposite faces add up to 7.
  4. Get every die onto a goal showing that goal's number on top.
  5. Stuck? Undo as often as you like, or Restart.
