How to play

This was a solved Suguru. A few rows and columns slipped. Slide them back.

A region of N cells must hold 1 to N once each. The same number may never touch another, not even diagonally.

Regions turn green when they hold the right numbers. Red rings mark equal numbers that touch.

Drag a row sideways or a column up and down; numbers wrap around the edge. Or tap a number and use the arrows. Each step a line moves is one slide.

Keys: arrows move the cursor, Shift+arrow (or Space, then arrow) slides its line. Z undo · R restart · H hint · N next.
