# Suguru Shift

## The idea
> Suguru Shift: a solved Suguru, scrambled by Loopover-style row and column slides, that you put back using only
> the Suguru rules as the clue
> (studio pitch, 26 Oct 2026 — Benji's queue was empty and Pip Push had its 3 nights. Runners-up: "Wall-less
> Suguru", Fillomino-style, where you draw the regions yourself; and "Sum Trail", draw one path through every cell
> where checkpoints show the running total you must hit.)

A finished Suguru whose numbers have been knocked out of place by a few wrapping row and column slides; you slide
lines back until the grid is a valid Suguru again, in as few slides as you can.

Why this one: it is Benji's favourite puzzle family with a twist that turns "fill in" into "repair", every slide is
a small deduction (which line moved, and which way), and it fits one night because levels can be generated and
proven by search. (Shift Suguru was a runner-up in the Pip Push pitch; "Wall-less Suguru" needs a much heavier
drawing UI on the phone.)

## References
- No links in the pitch. Loopover (row/column wrap-sliding puzzle) and Suguru/Tectonic rules are known; nothing fetched.

## Tonight (night 1)
1. Core: fixed regions, numbers that slide with wrap-around, live rule feedback (regions turn green when they hold
   1..N, red rings on equal numbers that touch, also diagonally), win on any valid Suguru.
2. 12 generated levels, 4×4 to 6×6, par 1 to 6, each proven: no valid grid within par−1 slides and exactly one
   valid grid at par.
3. Drag a row or column on touch/mouse; tap a number + slide pad; keyboard cursor + Shift/Space+arrows.
4. Undo, instant restart, hint (next slide towards the solution), level picker with best results.

## Assumptions
- Any valid Suguru counts as a win, not only the original grid (at par the original is the only one reachable).
- Par = fewest slides; a slide is one step; sliding a row two steps counts as two slides.
- All levels open from the start; the picker marks solved and at-par levels.

## Later
- Daily generated board; bigger 7×7 boards (needs a faster solver for par proofs).
- Locked lines (rows that can't move), or numbers that are pinned to their cell.
- Hints that explain the deduction instead of showing the move.

## Design
### Core loop
Look at which regions are broken and where equal numbers touch, guess which row or column slipped, slide it, see
the colours update. Regions are drawn on the board and never move; only the numbers ride along with a slide, and
the number that falls off one edge comes back on the other.

### The twist
Suguru rules are the only clue. A region of N cells must hold 1..N, so a 4 in a 3-region or two 2s in one region
point at a slipped line; touching equal numbers (also diagonally) point at it too. The deduction is about lines,
not cells.

### Controls
- Touch/mouse: drag a row sideways or a column up/down. The line follows the finger with wrap-around and snaps to
  whole steps on release (each step is one slide).
- Tap a number to select it, then the ←↑↓→ pad slides its row/column one step (one-hand phone play).
- Keyboard: arrows move the cursor; Shift+arrow (or Space then arrow) slides the cursor's line; Z / Backspace undo,
  R restart, H hint, N next level.

### Progression
Generated by `lab/gen.mjs` (seed 2026) and proven by iterative-deepening search over canonical slide sequences:
1–2 one slide (row, then column), 3 two slides on 4×4, 4–7 5×5 with 2–3 slides, 8–12 6×6 with 3–6 slides. Every
level has exactly one valid grid at par. The 6×6 levels also keep at least 3 regions intact, at most 12 touching
cells, and a correct first slide among the 3 that improve the board most (so the colours stay a usable clue). Tips on the first levels teach: drag a row, a column, green regions,
red rings, "a region of 3 holds 1, 2, 3".

### Win / lose / restart
Win when the grid is valid: card with slides vs par and Next. No lose state; Undo is unlimited and Restart is
instant (and undoable).

### Feel
- The line slides smoothly with wrap ghosts; snapped slides animate in 120 ms (off with reduced motion).
- Regions flash green the moment they become correct; a short chord on a win (WebAudio, mute toggle).
- At-par wins get a star in the picker.

### QA plan
- `state()`: `{screen: "playing"|"won", level, slides, par, grid, valid, regionsOk, regionsTotal, clashes}`.
- Actions: `"r<row>L"`, `"r<row>R"`, `"c<col>U"`, `"c<col>D"`, plus `"undo"` and `"restart"`.
- `solve()`: bidirectional search from the current grid to the level's goal grid (up to 10 slides), else
  `["restart", ...stored solution]`. Every level must finish with it.
