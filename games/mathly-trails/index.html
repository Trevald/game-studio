How to play

Make the target with an expression that uses all five cards once each. + − × ÷ and parentheses are free to use as often as you like.

Tap a card or operator to place it at the caret, or drag it into a gap. Drag a placed token to move it, or back to the cards to take it out. Tap a placed token for Before, After and Remove.

Long expressions continue on the next page; use the arrows above the expression.

Your result updates as you go. The puzzle finishes by itself when all five cards make the target.

Keyboard: ←→ caret · +-*/() insert · number keys place a card · Backspace remove · Enter select · PgUpPgDn pages · Ctrl+Z or U undo · R restart · H hint · Esc back
