How to play

Every number is already in place; the walls are gone. Group the cells back into regions: a region of N cells holds each number from 1 to N once (so its biggest number is its size, at most 5).

Drag across cells to make a region. Drag from a region to grow it; drag back to take a cell out of the stroke. Tap a free cell to make a one-cell region, tap a cell in a region to free it.

Orange lines are fixed walls: a region never crosses one. A region turns green when it holds 1 to N. Solve the board by making every region green. There is exactly one answer, and it can be found without guessing.

Keyboard: arrows move · Space/Enter pen down/up · Backspace frees a cell · Z undo · R restart · H hint · N next · L levels
